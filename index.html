How a project runs

Four stages, with regular progress updates from first meeting to handover.

  1. Consultation

    We meet to understand your vision, assess the site, and agree the scope and budget.

  2. Design and planning

    Our architects produce detailed drawings and our engineers build a full project plan.

  3. Construction

    Skilled craftsmen carry out the build under strict quality control, with regular updates to you.

  4. Handover

    Final inspections and quality checks, then handover with complete project documentation.
