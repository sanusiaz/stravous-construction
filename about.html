About Stravous Construction

Building Nigeria’s future, one project at a time.

From a small team of engineers to a trusted name.

Stravous Construction began as a small group of dedicated engineers. Since 2013 it has grown into one of Nigeria’s respected construction firms, with work that ranges from luxury residential estates to large commercial developments.

What has not changed is how we work: quality craftsmanship, transparent processes and a commitment to every client we build for.
